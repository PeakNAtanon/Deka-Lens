# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are legal interns and junior legal researchers who need to find relevant Thai Supreme Court judgments quickly while learning how to frame legal issues. They use the tool during research tasks, case preparation, and internal training.

## Product Purpose

Deka Lens helps interns build better Thai legal search queries, run those queries across Thai judgment sources, compare results, and keep short research notes. Success means a new intern can move from a broad legal question to candidate judgment numbers and usable issue notes with less supervision.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, and instructional. The interface should feel like a calm research desk: practical, precise, and supportive without pretending to give legal advice.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-first legal landing pages, decorative dashboards, vague AI assistant promises, and dense expert-only tools that hide the next action. Avoid implying that search results are official legal advice or verified holdings before the user checks the source.

## Design Principles

- Put the intern's next research action first.
- Make source reliability and fallback behavior visible.
- Teach legal issue framing through the workflow, not long explanations.
- Keep the interface dense enough for real work but calm enough for beginners.
- Separate fetched results, external-search fallbacks, and user notes clearly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-friendly controls. Preserve Thai readability with clear labels, sufficient line height, and meaningful link text. Respect reduced motion and avoid color-only status indicators.
